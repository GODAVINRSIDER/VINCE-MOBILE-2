BIG ROUND - copy text, staged images, flexible vision, swipe-to-reply, real deep research
=================================================================================================

EXTRACTION - same as always: extract straight into your project folder,
choose overwrite. Confirm ImageStore.kt and DeepResearch.kt both exist.

1. COPY/SELECT TEXT - fixed. Long-press any message to select and copy,
   same as any normal Android text now.

2. IMAGE FLOW REDESIGNED - tap Camera/Screen/Files now just STAGES the
   image (shown as a small preview above the input, with an X to
   cancel) instead of instantly sending it for analysis. Type a caption
   or question, or leave it blank and just tap send.

3. VISION NO LONGER ASSUMES "TRADING CHART" - this is what caused the
   DAVINA screenshot mix-up. It now describes what's actually in the
   image first, and only gives the chart-style read if it genuinely IS
   a chart. Applies everywhere: in-chat and the floating widget.

4. IMAGES NOW ACTUALLY PERSIST - every photo/screenshot sent is saved
   in the app's own storage and stays visible in the chat as a
   thumbnail, permanently - not just a "[Photo]" placeholder with the
   image gone. Honest limit: this makes the image visible/referenceable
   again, but a later text-only follow-up won't automatically re-run
   vision on it - what carries forward is VINCE's own description of it
   in the conversation history (already fixed earlier).

5. SWIPE TO REPLY - swipe any message right (WhatsApp/Instagram style)
   to reply directly to it - shows a preview above the input, and the
   AI gets told explicitly what you're replying to.

6. REAL DEEP RESEARCH - "research X" / "look into X" / "investigate X"
   now runs a genuine multi-step process: the AI breaks the topic into
   3 angles, runs a real deeper search on each, then synthesizes a
   thorough answer - not one shallow single search. Needs your Tavily
   key in Settings (same one added earlier).
