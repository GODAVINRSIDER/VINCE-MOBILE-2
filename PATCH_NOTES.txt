VINCE MOBILE 2.3 - forced live search, continuous voice, image + candle drawing, guarded reminders
===============================================================================================
Extract into your project folder and overwrite. app/debug.keystore is the SAME key as before
- do not replace it. versionCode 4 / versionName 2.3-phone.

NEW IN 2.3
1. FORCED LIVE SEARCH (Tavily). The model no longer decides whether to search.
   - Words like search, look up, research, latest, current, now, today, news, update, outlook,
     forecast, price of... ALWAYS search.
   - Every real question, "analyze/give me/explain..." request, or message of 5+ words also
     searches. Only tiny small talk (hi, ok, thanks) and local commands (time, reminders, phone
     actions) skip it.
   - Short follow-ups ("and silver?") are glued to your previous message so the search has a subject.
   - Prompts rewritten: no more mention of training data / knowledge cutoff, no 2024-and-earlier
     history unless you ask. If search fails it says "couldn't pull live data" instead of guessing.
2. CONTINUOUS VOICE (in the app AND the floating widget). Tap once; it listens, answers, listens
   again. Ends on "end convo", "I'm done", "that's all", "stop listening", "goodbye", a tap on the
   mic/widget, or 3 minutes of silence (it says it is going on standby). Works on the speaker or
   with earphones. You can cut in while it talks (say "stop"/"wait" or just start your question);
   chat also has Interrupt and a Cut-in on/off chip. On loud speaker some phones echo, so use the
   chip if it ever interrupts itself.
3. IMAGES. Shows which provider made the picture and why better ones were skipped. Your prompt is
   first rewritten into a detailed one. Looser wording is recognised ("I want an image of...",
   "show me a picture of...", "make me a logo for...").
4. CANDLE + SMC DRAWER (drawn by code, always accurate): morning/evening star, bullish/bearish
   engulfing, hammer, hanging man, shooting star, inverted hammer, doji (+dragonfly, gravestone),
   three white soldiers, three black crows, harami, piercing line, dark cloud cover, tweezer
   top/bottom, marubozu, spinning top, FVG, order block, BOS, CHoCH, liquidity sweep.
   Say e.g. "draw a bearish engulfing" or "show me a bullish FVG".
5. REMINDERS NOW FIRE IN THE BACKGROUND. Two independent delivery paths (alarm + a silent
   background guard service); whichever fires first delivers, no duplicates. Louder alarm-style
   channel. Settings > Alerts: "Keep VINCE awake in background" (on by default) and an
   "Autostart page" button that opens your phone maker's autostart screen.
   PHONE SETUP STILL NEEDED ONCE: Autostart ON for VINCE, Battery = No restrictions, lock VINCE
   in recent apps. No app can override a phone that force-stops it.


v2.3 FIXES (round 2, after first on-phone test)
- Persistent "Keeping alerts on time" notification REMOVED. A background guard notification now only
  exists while a reminder is pending and says exactly which one ("Reminder pending for 6:40 PM: ...").
- AI image generation REMOVED (low quality). Code-drawn candle/SMC patterns stay.
- VOICE REPLY BUG FIXED: the spoken turn crashed silently on a Compose scroll call that cannot run
  inside the voice session, so VINCE never answered by voice. Errors now show in chat.
- Beeps: recognizer earcons are muted for the session; ONE beep when the conversation starts and ONE
  when it ends. (Some phones refuse stream muting - tell me if beeps remain.)
- Phone actions: "open whatsapp and message <name> saying <text>" now works (contact lookup by name,
  needs Contacts permission, asked once; you press send). "message <name> <text>" also works when
  the name matches a saved contact. "open X and <something else>" opens X and says honestly it
  cannot do the rest inside other apps. Without permission it opens WhatsApp's picker with text filled.

VINCE MOBILE 2.4 - PHONE CONTROL (Accessibility), notification reply, MT5 trade rails
=====================================================================================
versionCode 5 / 2.4-phone. Same keystore.

SETUP (once): VINCE > Settings > Phone control.
 1. Accessibility > VINCE phone control > ON. If Android blocks it ("restricted setting"):
    App info > three dots > Allow restricted settings, then try again.
 2. Notification access > VINCE > ON (to read messages and use Reply).
 3. Optional: MetaTrader 5 trade execution switch (OFF by default). Use a DEMO account first.

WHAT IT DOES
- "phone: <anything>" or natural commands: "open youtube and search gold analysis", "play X on spotify",
  "turn on bluetooth", "set an alarm for 6am". VINCE reads the screen and taps/types step by step.
- "what's on my screen" / "summarize this page": switch to the app; it reads 5 seconds later.
- "dump screen": sends the raw screen text (used to calibrate MetaTrader).
- "read my messages": reads WhatsApp/SMS/Telegram/Gmail notifications.
- "reply to John saying ok": uses the notification's own Reply box (confirm first).
- "message John saying hi": opens the chat prefilled; say "send it" and it presses Send.
- MT5: "place a buy on gold 0.01 lots SL 4510 TP 4560 on MT5" -> VINCE repeats the numbers, waits for
  your yes, fills the ticket, VERIFIES every number on screen, then taps Buy/Sell.
  After a chart analysis card: "take that trade 0.01 lots". Add "no need to confirm" to skip the yes.
  Rails: switch must be ON, lots <= your cap (default 0.02, "set mt5 max lot to 0.05"), SL and TP
  required and on the correct sides, 1 trade per minute, never invents lot/SL/TP, refuses if the
  screen doesn't match.
- Stop: say "stop", tap the widget, tap STOP on the "VINCE is controlling your phone" notification,
  or the STOP button in Settings.
- Every send/pay/post/delete/buy/sell tap waits for "yes". Screen text is treated as data, never orders.
