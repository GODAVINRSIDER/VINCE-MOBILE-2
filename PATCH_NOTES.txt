VINCE MOBILE 2.5 - SPEED ROUND (versionCode 6 / versionName 2.5-phone)
======================================================================
Extract into your project folder and overwrite (zip root is app/ - no wrapper folder).
app/debug.keystore is untouched - do not replace it.

GOAL: first words in ~1-2 seconds instead of 4-5, and no more waiting 10s on a dead provider.

WHAT CHANGED
1. GEMINI THINKING OFF (minimal). Gemini 3.6 Flash thinks by default, which was most of the old
   4-5s wait. Chat now sends thinkingLevel "minimal"; if Google ever rejects that, it steps down
   to thinkingBudget 0, then to no setting, and remembers (GeminiClient.kt).
2. PROVIDER RACE instead of one-after-another (BrainRouter.kt). The fastest healthy provider
   starts at once; if it has produced nothing after 1.3s the next starts alongside it (3.2s for
   the third), or immediately if one fails. First to answer wins, the others are cut off.
   Old behaviour: wait for Gemini to fail (+1.5s retry), then Groq, then OpenRouter.
3. SELF-TUNING ORDER + CIRCUIT BREAKER (ProviderHealth.kt). Remembers each provider's recent
   speed and skips one that failed in the last 45s. Resets when the app restarts.
4. GROQ reasoning_effort "low" (gpt-oss-120b reasons before answering; low makes it near-instant).
5. STREAMING in the typed chat. The reply appears word by word in a live bubble, and (if
   replies are spoken) VINCE starts talking on the first finished sentence.
   Voice conversation, widget and Home mic still wait for the whole reply, but now get the
   faster providers + race.
6. TIGHTER TIMEOUTS: connect 6s, no sleep-and-retry on the chat path, 14s overall cap (45s for
   research). Tavily lookups: 4s cap, and no slow second search after a timeout.
7. LEANER PROMPT: history sent to the AI is now the last 24 messages (older ones shortened to
   350 chars, last 4 in full) instead of 60. Everything is still stored on-device.
   To go back: MAX_HISTORY_MESSAGES in BrainRouter.kt.
8. VOICE: end-of-speech wait cut from ~2.2s to ~1.3s (VoiceSession.kt) so your question is sent sooner.

FILES: ProviderHealth.kt (new), HttpStream.kt (new), GeminiClient.kt, OpenAiCompatibleClient.kt,
BrainRouter.kt, WebSearchTool.kt, VoiceOutput.kt, ChatScreen.kt, VoiceSession.kt, app/build.gradle.kts

EXPECTED TIMINGS (rough): no-search question ~1-2s to first words; question that triggers a
live search ~2.5-3.5s (Tavily adds 1-2s).

If the voice cuts you off mid-sentence, raise the two SILENCE_LENGTH numbers in VoiceSession.kt.
