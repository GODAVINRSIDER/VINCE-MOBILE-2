STAGE 15 (first slice) + carried-forward fixes
=================================================

EXTRACTION - same as always: extract straight into your project folder
(the one with .git already in it), choose overwrite when asked, confirm
app/src/main/java/com/godavin/vince/OverlayService.kt exists afterward.

WHAT'S NEW THIS ROUND
------------------------
1. FLOATING MIC WIDGET (Stage 15, first working slice)
   - Home screen now has a "Turn on floating widget" button. First tap
     asks for the one-time "display over other apps" permission; tap
     again after granting it to actually start the widget.
   - A small draggable colored bubble appears on top of whatever app
     you're in, showing the first letter of the active persona.
   - TAP it: starts listening immediately (same mic as in-app), routes
     what you say through the same command pipeline as normal chat,
     speaks the reply out loud - no need to open VINCE first.
   - HOLD it (half a second): cycles VINCE -> CLARA -> DAVINA, updates
     the bubble's color/letter and confirms out loud.
   - DRAG it: moves anywhere on screen, stays there.
   - A "VINCE is running" notification stays up while the widget is
     active - this is an Android requirement for any background service,
     not something that can be hidden.
   - NOT YET IN THIS SLICE: camera vision and screen vision from the
     floating widget - both need a full Activity to request their
     system permissions, so they're a fast follow-up once this slice is
     confirmed working. Voice + persona-switch is what's testable now.
   - Force-closing VINCE removes the widget entirely, as expected.

2. VOICE PICKER STILL NOT WORKING - added diagnostics, not a full fix
   yet since this needs your report. In Settings, under "System checks",
   there's now a button to jump straight to your phone's Text-to-Speech
   engine settings. Most likely cause: your phone's current speech
   engine doesn't expose multiple selectable voices at all (common on
   some budget-phone default engines) - if so, installing/selecting
   Google's Text-to-Speech engine as the system default should populate
   the list. Try this and tell me what the voice list actually shows
   (empty? one entry? several?) so the next patch can target the real
   cause instead of guessing again.

3. REMINDERS STILL ONLY FIRING WHILE APP OPEN - also added diagnostics.
   Settings > System checks now shows whether battery-optimization
   exemption is actually granted (it may not be, if you dismissed that
   prompt) plus a button to open it directly. There's also a button to
   jump to VINCE's app-details screen, since some phone brands hide a
   separate "Autostart" toggle there that Android itself has no way to
   trigger automatically. Check both, and tell me your phone brand/model
   if reminders still don't fire in the background afterward - that's
   the fastest way to get you the exact right toggle instead of guessing.
