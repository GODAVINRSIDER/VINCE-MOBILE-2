VINCE MOBILE 2.1 - big round: vision fix, alerts, briefing, reminders, images, trade card
==========================================================================================
Extract into your project folder (overwrite). Confirm app/debug.keystore exists.

FIRST INSTALL OF THIS BUILD NEEDS ONE LAST UNINSTALL (old builds were signed with a random
key each time). Before uninstalling: Settings > Backup. API keys and PIN are not in the
backup - copy your keys somewhere first. After this build, updates install over the top.

1. VISION: Gemini retries 503 and falls through to other flash models; Groq model is found
   live (old llama-4-scout was shut down); OpenRouter tries several free vision models.
   Errors are short and readable, not saved to chat, not spoken.
2. ALERTS (Settings > Alerts and briefings): London / New York / Asia open, high-impact
   news heads-up, daily briefing, journal check-in. Each has its own switch.
3. BRIEFING: sessions in your time, today's high-impact news, headlines (needs Tavily key),
   your rules and today's plan. Say "briefing" in chat for it on demand.
4. RULES / PLAN: "my rules: ...", "today's plan: ...", "show my rules", "clear today's plan".
5. REMINDERS back: "remind me at 3pm to ...", "remind me in 20 minutes to ...",
   "list my reminders", "cancel my reminders". Re-armed on app open and after reboot.
6. GREETING: time-of-day greeting only at the start of a conversation (or after 6h quiet).
7. IMAGES: "generate an image of ..." with Copy / Save / Share buttons.
8. TRADE-IDEA CARD under chart analyses (entry, stop, target, R:R calculated in code).
9. PHONE ACTIONS: "open tradingview.com", "google ...", "set a timer for 10 minutes",
   "navigate to ...".
10. Personas now know what the app can and can't do.
