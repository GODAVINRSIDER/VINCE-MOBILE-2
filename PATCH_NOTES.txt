SETTINGS SCREEN FIX + GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP/RESTORE
=====================================================

EXTRACTION - same as always: extract straight into your project folder,
choose overwrite.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
---------------------------
Found the real bug behind the screenshot: the Settings screen never had
scroll support added, even as it grew to 4 stacked sections (API keys,
System checks, Security, Backup). Once that content got taller than
your screen, everything past the bottom edge was being clipped - that's
what made your API keys look empty (they were still saved fine, just
scrolled out of the visible/reachable area) and what produced that
stray line through "Battery optimization" (a text field's border
getting cut off exactly at the screen edge, not an actual strikethrough).

FIXES
--------
1. Added proper scrolling to the whole Settings screen - every section
   is reachable now, nothing gets clipped.
2. Grouped each section into its own dark bordered card (API keys,
   System checks, App lock, Backup & restore) instead of a long
   continuous list separated by thin dividers - clearer visual
   separation, matches the card style already used on the Dashboard.
3. Backup/restore now has two clearly separate buttons like you asked:
   - "Backup to Google Drive" tries to open Drive directly with your
     backup file ready to upload. If Drive isn't installed, it falls
     back to the normal share sheet so it never dead-ends.
   - "Restore from Google Drive" opens the file picker, which already
     lists Drive as a browsable source right alongside your phone's own
     storage - so you can pick a backup straight out of Drive.

TESTING CHECKLIST
--------------------
1. Open Settings - confirm you can scroll all the way down and every
   section (API keys, System checks, App lock, Backup) is in its own
   card, fully visible, nothing clipped or overlapping.
2. Confirm your API keys are still there (not actually lost - just
   re-enter and Save if they do show blank, to be safe).
3. Tap "Backup to Google Drive" - confirm Drive opens (or the share
   sheet, if Drive isn't installed) with a real file ready to save.
4. Tap "Restore from Google Drive" - confirm the file picker shows
   Drive as one of the browsable locations.
