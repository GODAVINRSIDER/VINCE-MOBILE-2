FIX: "OPEN <APP>" COULDN'T FIND ANY APP
============================================

EXTRACTION - same as always: extract straight into your project folder,
choose overwrite.

THE REAL CAUSE
------------------
Since Android 11, apps can't see other installed apps through the
system's PackageManager by default - Google calls this "package
visibility," meant to stop apps from silently fingerprinting everything
else on your phone. This app targets a recent enough Android version
that it's been hit by that restriction the whole time AppLauncher
existed - it wasn't broken logic, it was straight up not allowed to see
WhatsApp (or anything else) in the first place, so "couldn't find" was
technically true from the code's point of view.

THE FIX
-----------
Added a <queries> declaration to the manifest, specifically asking for
visibility into any app that has a launcher icon (i.e. anything that
shows up in your app drawer) - narrow, exactly what's needed, nothing
broader. Also switched AppLauncher.kt to query using that same intent
directly (queryIntentActivities) instead of the older method, which is
the reliable, correct pairing for this.

TESTING CHECKLIST
--------------------

2. Try 2-3 other installed apps by name to confirm it's not a one-off.
