VINCE MOBILE 2.2 - alert timing fix + phone actions (contains everything from 2.1 too)
=====================================================================================
Extract into your project folder and overwrite. app/debug.keystore is the SAME key as 2.1
- do not replace or regenerate it, or the next install will refuse to update over the old one.

NEW IN 2.2
- Session alerts now work out their wording from the real clock. If the phone delivers an alert
  late, it says "session already open, N min ago" instead of claiming "in about 10 min".
- Settings > Alerts: "Recent alerts (due vs arrived)" shows how late each alert was, plus
  battery-optimization status. Use it to see whether lateness is consistent.
- Phone actions (no special access needed): flashlight on/off, volume up/down/mute/set %,
  music play/pause/next/previous, battery level, dial a number, prefilled SMS and WhatsApp
  drafts, shortcuts into Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / battery / display / sound / airplane / location
  settings, "turn on/off wifi|bluetooth|mobile data|airplane mode" (opens the switch).
- Personas know about the new actions.

PHRASES
  flashlight on | flashlight off | torch
  volume up | volume down | louder | quieter | mute | unmute | volume 40
  pause music | play music | next song | previous song
  battery | what's my battery level
  call 0712345678 | text 0712345678 saying running late | whatsapp 0712345678 saying hi
  open wifi settings | turn on bluetooth | turn off wifi
  set a timer for 10 minutes | set a 10 minute timer
