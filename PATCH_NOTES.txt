TIME-OF-DAY AWARENESS + SUMMARY-STYLE CHAT TITLES
========================================================

EXTRACTION - same as always: extract straight into your project folder,
choose overwrite. Confirm app/src/main/java/com/godavin/vince/
TitleGenerator.kt exists afterward (new file).

1. TIME-OF-DAY AWARENESS
   Same mechanism as the date fix - every message now also tells the AI
   the real time of day off your phone's clock (morning/afternoon/
   evening/late night), so a natural "good morning"/"good evening" can
   happen when a greeting is actually called for (start of a
   conversation, etc) - not forced into every single reply, just aware
   of it.

2. CHAT TITLES - NOW SUMMARY-STYLE
   New chats used to just take your first typed sentence, truncated at
   40 characters, as the title. Now, right after a brand-new thread's
   first exchange, a quick background request summarizes what the
   conversation was actually about into a short 3-6 word title (e.g.
   "Gold 15m Breakout Setup" instead of whatever you happened to type
   first) - happens after the reply, so it never slows anything down,
   and only touches a thread's title if it's still on the auto default
   (never overwrites a title you set yourself).

3. RENAME A CHAT BY COMMAND
   Type or say "rename this chat to [name]" / "call this chat [name]" /
   "title this chat [name]" inside any chat, and it renames that thread
   directly - no AI call needed, instant, and confirms back to you.

ON YOUR "IS IT NOW UP TO DATE" QUESTION - important distinction
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Worth being precise here since it matters: this fixes DATE AWARENESS -
VINCE will no longer reason like it's still 2024/2025, and now has a
sense of morning/evening too. But it does NOT give the underlying AI
model new knowledge of things that happened after its own training
cutoff. The model's actual factual knowledge is still frozen wherever
its training data ended - knowing today's real date doesn't retroactively
teach it about news, prices, or events since then. For anything that
genuinely needs to be live (today's news, current prices), the app
needs a dedicated tool that actually fetches that data in the moment -
which is exactly what the time/date/economic-calendar features already
do (and what price-fetching used to do before it got removed). So:
date-aware now, yes. Automatically knowledgeable about anything that
happened after each model's training cutoff, no - that would need a
real-time fetch tool built for that specific thing, same pattern as
what's already there for the calendar.
