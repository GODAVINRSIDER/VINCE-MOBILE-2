WHAT'S FIXED/ADDED THIS ROUND
--------------------------------

1. LOGO SHAPE FIXED
   Redrew the triangle mark wider and shallower (apex pulled further
   down, top corners pulled wider) so the outer/inner nested triangles
   actually read as a "V" shape like the reference image, instead of two
   plain equilateral triangles. Same file (ic_vince_triangle.xml), same
   spot on the floating widget.

2. VINCE VOICE - NOW A DROPDOWN
   Settings > VINCE voice is now a single dropdown field instead of the
   scrollable list. Tap it, pick a voice from the list - it previews out
   loud AND saves as VINCE's voice in that one tap. No pitch/rate
   controls shown anywhere - just "which voice."

3. CAMERA + SCREEN VISION NOW WORK FROM THE FLOATING WIDGET
   This completes Stage 15 - the floating widget can now do everything
   the in-app chat can:
   - ONE finger, quick tap: voice command (unchanged)
   - ONE finger, hold half a second: switch persona (unchanged)
   - TWO fingers, quick tap together: camera vision - opens the camera,
     analyzes the photo with the same chart-reading prompt as in-app
     Camera, speaks the answer out loud
   - TWO fingers, hold together half a second: screen vision - same
     3-second "switch to what you want read" warning as in-app Screen,
     then captures and speaks the answer
   Both are genuinely invisible - no extra screen appears, just the
   camera app opening (for camera) or the same warning toast (for
   screen), then VINCE speaks the result and you're right back to
   whatever you were doing. Two-finger gestures were used specifically
   so they add zero delay to the everyday single-finger voice tap.
